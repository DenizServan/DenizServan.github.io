Greenhouse IoT Platform

A monitoring and control platform for greenhouses. One STM32-based board combines CAN bus, 868 MHz RF, Wi-Fi and LTE with power management and relay control for the actuators, designed for an IP67 enclosure.

Python · RAI · PhysX

Two Robots Spell a Word

Two Franka Panda arms on mobile bases read letter and digit pieces from a pile on the floor, fetch the ones they need and spell a word across two tables. A camera with a CNN reads each piece's symbol and angle; KOMO plans the arm motions and A* moves the bases.
